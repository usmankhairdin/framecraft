              What belongs in a photography brief?⌄
            
            

              

                The subject, intended audience, where the images will appear,
                essential viewpoints or product details, and any practical
                constraints. A reference image can help explain a direction
                without prescribing an exact copy.
              

            

          

          

            
              Are the images here commissioned Framecraft work?⌄
            
            

              

                No. Framecraft is a website template. These are illustrative
                stock photographs, with source links and licences supplied in
                the package. Replace them with work you have permission to
                present before launching your own portfolio.
              

            

          

          

            
              Can I save a reference for my enquiry?⌄
            
            

              

                Choose “Use as reference” below a photograph or inside the
                viewer. It becomes part of your unsent enquiry draft. The page
                does not upload or send a photograph, and it does not grant a
                licence to use that image outside this template.
              

            

          

          

            
              Does the draft request a quote or reserve a shoot?⌄
            
            

              

                No. It is a local text draft you can download. No message is
                sent, no quote is generated and no booking is made. Contact a
                photographer directly to agree a real project.
